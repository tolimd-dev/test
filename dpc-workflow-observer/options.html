AI analysis (OpenAI API)

Optional but powerful. Without a key, the extension still finds common workflow patterns. With a key, it sends your anonymized workflow sequences (no patient names, no URLs) to GPT-4o for deeper, personalized suggestions.

Get a key at platform.openai.com → API keys. A few cents per analysis session. Patient data is never sent — only tool names and sequences.
Not configured

Your key is stored only in chrome.storage.local on this device. It is never synced to Google or any other server.

HIPAA: Your OpenAI BAA covers Wren's use of the API. Wren's background analysis is already anonymized (no patient names or identifiers). With your BAA in place, you can also type patient context directly into chat.

GPT-4o mini is fine for most workflow conversations. Use GPT-4o if you want Wren to reason through more complex design tradeoffs.
